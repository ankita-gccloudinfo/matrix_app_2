Law & Order — Unrest Early Warning

Detects mobilisation & unrest before it forms
What this module actually does It watches how fast a "story" is spreading on social media compared to that story's own normal pace, and warns an officer before a crowd forms — not after. Every batch of similar posts about the same real event is grouped into one topic. Every few minutes the system counts how many new posts that topic gained in the last 5 / 10 / 15 / 30 / 60 minutes (topic_velocity_snapshots) and compares that speed to the topic's own 7-day-average speed (topic_baseline_metrics). If a topic is growing many times faster than its own baseline and is spreading across multiple platforms, the system writes an alert (viral_alerts) with a severity level and pushes it out.

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT va.primary_district) AS active_high_risk_zones
FROM viral_alerts va
JOIN topic t ON t.unique_topic_id = va.unique_topic_id
WHERE va.alert_level IN ('HIGH','CRITICAL')
  AND va.created_at >= NOW() - INTERVAL 24 HOUR
  AND t.broad_category LIKE '%Law and Order%'
  AND (t.topic_status IS NULL OR t.topic_status NOT IN ('Resolved','Closed'));

Women's Safety Intelligence

Separates real distress signals from helpline perception
What this module actually does It separates two very different things that both look like "a post about women's safety": someone describing an incident that may never reach a police station, and someone commenting on how the helpline or police responded. The first becomes a pre-FIR distress signal the department can proactively reach out on; the second becomes the perception score for 1090/181/112. This split matters because averaging them together would hide both.

     OR JSON_SEARCH(keywords_cloud,'one','1090') IS NOT NULL

Crime Intelligence & OSINT

Finds coordinated accounts behind a spike
What this module actually does Beyond reading text, the schema tracks who is posting and how accounts relate to each other — follower/following graphs (profile_network), account-quality signals like verified status and automation flags (post_users), and even face-matching across images (archive_media_faces). This module uses that to spot when a "spike" is actually a handful of coordinated accounts rather than genuine organic spread.

Entity-level sentiment toward the department
What this module actually does A post's overall sentiment can be negative about a crime while still being positive about how police handled it — so this module reads sentiment_entities instead, which stores a separate stance per named entity in a post (a department, a thana, an officer). That's the only correct source for "how people feel about us," as opposed to "how people feel about the news."

Geo & Language Intelligence

Normalises messy locations into clean districts
What this module actually does Free-text location mentions in a post ("near the old bus stand, Sitapur") aren't usable for district-level rollups on their own. location_normalizer is the schema's answer — it caches a normalised district/city/village + coordinates + confidence score for every location string seen before, backed by geonames_places and district_tehsil_village. Every district number in this system is only as good as that normalisation step.
